Knowledge

λ depends both on the process technology used to fabricate the device and on the channel length L that the circuit designer selects.

Specifically, the value of λ is much larger for newer submicron technologies than for older technologies. This makes intuitive sense:

Newer technologies have very short channels, and are therefore much more greatly impacted by the channel-length modulation effect.

Also, for a given process technology, λ is inversely proportional to L.
